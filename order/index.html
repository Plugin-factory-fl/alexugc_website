03 / PRICING FAQ

UGC creator pricing questions

How much does a UGC video cost?+

Custom videos are priced by length and quantity. Current per-video rates are $80 for 15s, $100 for 30s or 45s, $120 for 60s, $150 for 90s, and $160 for 120s. Multiply the selected length price by the number of videos (1-100). Fixed packages are $90 for 1 video, $250 for 3, and $420 for 5.

What do the packages include?+

Each package lists vertical 9:16 video, captions and licensed music, one revision round, and organic social usage. Paid-ad usage and other add-ons are scoped separately in writing.

How does ordering work?+

Select a package or custom length, open Google Forms with your order reference, submit the brief, then continue to Stripe hosted payment and paste the same reference.
