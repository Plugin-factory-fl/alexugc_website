06 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before we start.

Still deciding what you need?

Send me your questions ↗
What do you need to get started?+

Your product, audience, main message, and what you want viewers to do. The order steps collect your script or directions, reference links, and the email to use for the project. Stripe then collects payment.

How do physical products work?+

I'll need the product in hand before filming. Answer yes to the shipping question when you order, and I'll email you my current shipping address within 24 hours.

What's included in a revision round?+

Reasonable adjustments to captions, pacing, music, and the edit, consistent with the approved brief. New concepts, reshoots, and substantial changes to scope are quoted separately.

Can I use the videos in paid ads?+

Standard pricing covers organic social usage on your brand's own channels. Paid-ad usage, whitelisting, and extended licensing are available as separate add-ons to be agreed in writing.

Can you match my brand's style?+

Absolutely. Share examples of content you like, your brand guide, and any must-use language. I'll adapt the creative while keeping it natural and audience-first.

Can I order a custom project?+

Yes. If you need a different number of videos, multiple hooks, a series, or broader usage rights, send a project brief and I can scope it with you.
